## Rules for the room

- Answer the question asked, then stop. A 3-minute answer to a 30-second
  question costs the next question.
- "I don't know" plus how you would find out beats a confident invention.
- Never concede the ambition to make a questioner comfortable; defend the core
  with the fallback.
- One number per answer, and know where it comes from.
