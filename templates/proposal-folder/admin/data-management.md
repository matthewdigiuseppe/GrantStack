# Data management plan

`/draft-section data-management` drafts this. Most schemes (ERC, NWO) require a
DMP, often FAIR-aligned (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable).

Cover, briefly:

- What data/code/materials the project generates or reuses.
- Where they live during the project and where they are archived after.
- Access, licensing, embargoes, and any privacy/consent constraints.
- Standards and repositories (domain repository, Zenodo, DANS, etc.).
- Who is responsible and what it costs (the budget must reflect this).
